Services

Critical power services, end to end

We handle a critical-power asset across its whole life — specification and supply, installation and commissioning, routine maintenance, mid-life battery replacement, fault diagnosis and eventual replacement. Most clients start with one repair and stay on contract.

01 · 1 kVA – 200 kVA

UPS installation, commissioning and maintenance

From a 1 kVA rack unit protecting a BMS panel to a 200 kVA system carrying a resort's critical bus — we install, commission, maintain and repair uninterruptible power supplies across the UAE.

  • Supply of new systems, including EAST UPS as authorised UAE partner
  • Installation, cabling, relocation and start-up
  • Commissioning with functional and load testing
  • Preventive maintenance: capacitor and fan condition, thermal checks, connection torque, filter replacement, parameter review
  • Fault diagnosis and repair, including board-level assessment
  • Battery bank condition testing and replacement
  • Decommissioning and safe removal of legacy units
A UPS that has not been load-tested is an assumption, not a protection. We verify capacity rather than reading a front panel.

02 · 24 / 48 / 110 / 220 VDC

Industrial battery chargers and DC systems

Switchgear tripping, plant control, telecom and emergency systems run on DC. We supply, commission and maintain industrial chargers and DC systems at 24 V, 48 V, 110 V and 220 V.

  • Supply and installation of industrial chargers and SMPS
  • Commissioning, boost and float parameter setting, functional testing
  • Discharge and load testing of the complete DC system
  • Preventive maintenance and fault rectification
  • Ni-Cd, lead-acid and VRLA battery bank integration
Reference: commissioning of 2 × 110 VDC / 800 A Schaefer chargers with KL1000P Ni-Cd banks at Dubai International Airport, and Gutor 110 VDC charger start-up with discharge testing for Petrofac's SCP MS80 project.

03 · Ni-Cd · VRLA · Flooded lead acid

Battery testing, replacement and capacity verification

Batteries are the most common cause of UPS failure and the most commonly neglected part of the system. We test them properly and replace them before they let you down.

  • Impedance and capacity testing with battery analysers
  • Timed discharge testing against rated autonomy
  • Full bank replacement, safe disposal and recommissioning
  • Ni-Cd, VRLA and flooded lead-acid types
  • Year-on-year capacity trending for AMC clients
VRLA blocks in a Dubai plant room rarely deliver the design life printed on the label. Ambient temperature, ripple and float voltage all take their share. Test annually; plan replacement where measured capacity crosses 80%.

04 · 32 kW & 100 kW resistive

Load-bank testing for UPS, generators and battery banks

We own our test equipment. Two 100 kVA resistive load banks and additional smaller units let us verify performance on your site, on your schedule, without hiring in.

  • UPS full-load and step-load response
  • Battery autonomy against rated runtime
  • Generator load acceptance and block loading
  • DC system discharge performance
  • Integrated UPS + generator + transfer sequence testing
Deliverable: a written test report with measured values, ambient conditions, deviation from rated performance and a clear recommendation — suitable for handover documentation, insurance and audit.
