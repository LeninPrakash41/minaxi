Annual Maintenance Contracts

An AMC is an agreement about risk

Not a subscription. It is an agreement about how much risk you are willing to carry, and what response you need when the risk lands. We write one contract per site, sized to the load.

Process

How we scope a contract

01

Survey

We attend site and list every unit, its age, its battery condition and its criticality.

02

Risk profile

We agree what a failure costs you — in hours, in reputation, in regulation.

03

Schedule

Visit frequency, response times and inclusions are set to that profile, not to a package.

04

Contract

Fixed annual or multi-year price, written scope, named engineer.

05

Reporting

A written condition report after every visit and an annual battery trend summary.

Visit frequency by criticality
  • Low criticality1 visit / year
  • Medium criticality2 visits / year
  • High criticality4 visits / year

Every AMC starts at the survey stage — the schedule that comes out of it can look like any of these three, or something between them, depending on what a failure would actually cost you.

Typical inclusions

What a contract covers

  • 1 to 4 preventive maintenance visits per year
  • Priority on-call response for contracted sites
  • Full functional and thermal inspection
  • Battery condition testing and year-on-year trending
  • Cleaning, filter replacement and connection torque checks
  • Written report with photographs and recommendations
  • Discounted rates on parts, batteries and out-of-scope work
