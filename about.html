About

Twenty years, one specialism

Minaxi Electrical Works L.L.C. was licensed in Dubai in October 2004 and has done the same thing ever since: keep critical power systems working.

We deliberately stayed specialist. Rather than becoming a general contractor, we went deep on UPS, DC systems, industrial chargers and battery technology — the equipment that sits between the mains supply and everything a business cannot afford to lose. That focus is why clients call us about a twenty-year-old Chloride or a discontinued Meissner and get a straight answer rather than a replacement quotation.

We are owner-led and senior-staffed, supported by a trusted network of specialist subcontractors for scale work. Low overhead, direct accountability, and no distance between the person who quotes the job and the person who does it.
