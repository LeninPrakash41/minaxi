Why Minaxi

A place where technicians become true master engineers

At Minaxi, you will never be confined to a single proprietary brand or simple filter swapping. You will work on Socomec, Chloride, Liebert, APC, EAST, Gutor, and rare industrial rectifiers.

  • Direct mentorship: Work alongside senior leadership with 20+ years of uninterrupted UAE field experience.
  • Factory-grade tools: Hands-on access to 32 kW and 100 kW resistive load banks, Hioki impedance testers, and thermal imagers.
  • High-visibility projects: Care for critical buses at top 5-star hotels, international banks, government hospitals, and broadcast stations.
  • Stability & integrity: Fully DED-licensed, on-time monthly compensation, visa sponsorship, and UAE labor law compliance.
